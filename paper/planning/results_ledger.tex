\section{Read this first}

I read all 44 result files, the ten STAGE findings documents, the audit and narrative
documents, and the full code inventory. Three conclusions dominate everything else.

\textbf{One, the synthetic win is real but rests entirely on one channel.}
\texttt{COMPASS-wnet\_occ} at \G{8.645}~dB [8.415, 8.856] beats \texttt{radiounet} at
9.934 [9.678, 10.179] with non-overlapping intervals, $p=2.5\!\times\!10^{-52}$. But
plain \texttt{COMPASS-wnet} is 10.112, which \emph{loses} to RadioUNet. The margin is
the occlusion channel and nothing else. Your own audit already recorded that occlusion
conditioning is a known technique (FadeNet, PLNet, TGPP), so this cannot be sold as a
novel method.

\textbf{Two, most of the original innovations are measurably inert.} The results files
themselves record this. \texttt{order\_matters.json} carries the flag
\B{"order\_helps\_under\_correlated\_noise": false}. \texttt{active\_learned.json}
carries \B{"physics\_aware\_uncertainty\_helps": false}. In
\texttt{benchmark.json}, the ablations for order-blind, order-shuffle, no-device and
no-rcl are all non-significant at $p\ge0.108$. Only building map and transmitter
position matter.

\textbf{Three, there are data integrity problems that would not survive review.}
Section 2 lists them. Two are severe enough that any claim resting on them is currently
unsupported.

\subsection{The occlusion diagnostic, which is the real intellectual content}

Controlled single-variable run, \texttt{results/nlos\_gap/}, same backbone, 40 epochs,
identical seeds, occlusion channel on or off. This is the cleanest experiment in the
project.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrr@{}}
\toprule
& Without occlusion & With occlusion & $\Delta$ \\ \midrule
Free-unobserved RMSE & 11.699 & \G{9.455} & $-2.244$ \\
Line of sight & 7.113 & \G{3.556} & $-3.557$ \\
Non line of sight & 12.610 & \G{10.472} & $-2.138$ \\
Parameters & 6{,}993{,}601 & 6{,}994{,}033 & \G{$+432$} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{432 parameters halve the line-of-sight error.} Every architecture in the panel,
across six families and two orders of magnitude of design effort, plateaus in the same
NLoS band. That is the signature of a missing input rather than insufficient capacity,
and it is a statement about the problem rather than about our network. Framed as an
analysis result it is defensible, interesting, and immune to the ``occlusion is known''
objection, because the finding is precisely that the field has been scaling
architectures against a bottleneck a known input removes.

\subsection{Order, device and source}

Order, model-free gap filling over 130 real walks: true 6.986, shuffled 12.351, mean
baseline 9.921, $p=4.7\times10^{-23}$, lag-1 autocorrelation 0.984. Learned:
GRU 1.788 against order-blind 9.788. \textbf{Caveat that must be disclosed:} the
order-blind set model at 9.788 is \B{worse than the trivial mean baseline} at 9.758, and
the GRU has 100{,}737 parameters against the set model's 12{,}673, so the comparison
confounds order-awareness with capacity by a factor of 7.9.

Device: offsets are highly estimable, split-half correlation 0.778 to 0.890, with median
spread 4.12\,dB and maximum 27.77\,dB. But calibrating them buys \B{0.18\% to 2.04\%} in
reconstruction. The single exception is EC Parking floor 2, the floor with the largest
spread and fewest cells, where variance reduction reaches 0.512.

Source: only \G{17.9\%} of cells (12 of 67) pass the reliability gate, which validates
the transmitter-agnostic design. But the median $R^2$ across all cells is 0.31 against a
gate of 0.30, so the count is fragile to the threshold, and \B{there is no ground-truth
transmitter position anywhere}, so no localisation error can be claimed.

\section{The paper I would write}

\textbf{Title direction.} \emph{What Transfers? A Benchmark and Diagnosis of Radio Field
Reconstruction from Simulation to Real Crowdsensing.}

\textbf{Claim structure.} Not ``we built a better network''. Instead: (i) we benchmark
eighteen methods under one protocol and find every deep family plateaus at the same
non-line-of-sight error; (ii) we show the bottleneck is a missing geometric input, not
capacity, and that 432 parameters remove it; (iii) we release the first real
crowdsensed benchmark with dense ground truth and show the simulation ranking does not
survive transfer; (iv) we identify detection heterogeneity, not RSS offset, as the
mechanism that breaks cross-device generalisation.

This framing turns every current liability into evidence. Classical winning on real data
becomes a finding about the protocol. Occlusion being known becomes the point. The inert
innovations simply drop out of the claims.

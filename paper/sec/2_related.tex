\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Architectures for radio map estimation.}
The dominant line of work treats the problem as image-to-image translation from a
building raster and a transmitter marker to a dense field.
RadioUNet~\cite{levie2021radiounet} established the template with a cascade of two
U-Nets~\cite{ronneberger2015unet} trained on ray-traced
simulation~\cite{yapar2023dataset}, and PMNet~\cite{lee2024pmnet} refined the
encoder-decoder for pathloss prediction. Successors have substituted the backbone
in turn. Conditional adversarial training~\cite{zhang2023rmegan}, denoising
diffusion~\cite{wang2025radiodiff, jia2025rmdm},
transformers~\cite{li2025rmtransformer, fang2025radioformer}, state-space
models~\cite{jia2025radiomamba} and uncertainty-driven active
construction~\cite{lu2025uram} have each reported gains. We benchmark this lineage
under one protocol and find that the substitutions move line-of-sight error and
leave the dominant non-line-of-sight error largely where it was, which reframes the
sequence as variation against a fixed bottleneck.

\paragraph{Geometry as an input rather than an architecture.}
That propagation geometry helps is not a new claim, and we do not present the
occlusion channel as a novel mechanism. FadeNet~\cite{ratnam2021fadenet} and EM
DeepRay~\cite{bakirtzis2022emdeepray} supply ray-derived or visibility-derived
features to convolutional predictors, and model-aided
learning~\cite{thrane2020modelaided} and map-based
approaches~\cite{dempsey2025mapbased} inject analytic propagation structure for the
same reason. The underlying physics is older still, with knife-edge
diffraction~\cite{deygout1966knifeedge} and the uniform theory of
diffraction~\cite{kouyoumjian1974utd} giving the shadow-depth intuition that
\cref{eq:occ} approximates cheaply. Our contribution is not the channel but the
measurement of what it is worth relative to architecture, the finding that the two
are substitutes, and the demonstration that the input cannot survive removal of its
transmitter anchor.

\paragraph{Neural fields and RF.}
A parallel line represents the radio field continuously.
NeRF$^2$~\cite{zhao2023nerf2} adapts neural radiance fields to RF propagation,
RF-Diffusion~\cite{chi2024rfdiffusion} generates RF signals with diffusion, and
Gaussian splatting has been carried into the radio
domain~\cite{zhang2024rf3dgs, wen2025wrfgs}. These methods fit a scene rather than
generalising across scenes, which is a different problem from the one studied here,
but they share our premise that the geometry of the environment is the right
inductive bias. Measured channel corpora such as DICHASUS~\cite{euchner2021dichasus}
supply the real data these methods increasingly need.

\paragraph{Sparse-to-dense prediction in vision.}
The task is structurally a completion problem. Depth
completion~\cite{ma2018sparsetodense} recovers a dense map from sparse range
returns, and inpainting with irregular masks~\cite{liu2018partialconv} handles the
free-form holes that trajectory sampling produces. We take the uncertainty
machinery from the same tradition, using Monte Carlo
dropout~\cite{gal2016dropout} rather than
ensembles~\cite{lakshminarayanan2017ensembles} for cost, with split conformal
prediction~\cite{lei2018conformal, angelopoulos2023conformal} as the recalibration
reference. Diffusion baselines in our panel follow the standard
formulation~\cite{ho2020ddpm} and its accelerated
sampler~\cite{song2021ddim}.

\paragraph{Real indoor measurement.}
Indoor radio measurement has a long history in
localisation~\cite{bahl2000radar, youssef2005horus}, where device
heterogeneity~\cite{kjaergaard2011heterogeneous, zafari2019survey} is a recognised
obstacle. Public indoor corpora with reference-point structure
exist~\cite{bi2022sodindoorloc, klus2024tuji1, moayeri2016perfloc, moayeri2018perfloc2},
though they target fingerprinting rather than field reconstruction and none pairs a
dense ground-truth field with surveyed transmitter positions. Our measurements are
of the same kind, and \cref{sec:real} argues that the absence of a dense reference
field is precisely what prevents the real setting from testing the capability the
simulated benchmark rewards.
